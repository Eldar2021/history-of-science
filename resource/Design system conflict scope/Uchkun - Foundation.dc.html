1Uchkun · Foundation — mood board, tokens, timeline (mobile + desktop, both themes)

Assumptions. Organic leads: cream ground, warm ink, terracotta + sage, over-rounded pills and 28px+ card corners. The dark theme is Organic's own neutral-900 ink (warm, not blue-black) with the accent lifted to the 400 step for AAA. Caprasimo and Figtree carry no Cyrillic and no Ң Ө Ү, so the type roles are filled by Literata (display: years, eras, titles — and all buttons, as Organic puts its heading face on buttons) and Golos Text (body, tabular figures). Terracotta is the timeline line and primary action; sage is the second voice (era headers, "Builds on"). The eight discipline colors are muted to sit at the same OKLCH weight as the Organic ramps.

Event images are empty slots — drop your own. Explore-canvas variations come in the next round.

A river of light, not a chart. Ancient centuries are quiet and sparse; the present is dense and bright. The wordmark ends in a single point of light — the spark — and nothing else glows. Restraint is the brand: one accent, one rule of light, warm paper or warm night.
